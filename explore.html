Explore the Coast

How to Use This Page

Explore the Biophilic Environmental Quality Index (BEQI) across Khao Lak by calculating live satellite data for any custom zone.

  1. 1

    Trace Your Boundary

    Click on the map to trace the boundary of the land you want to check the BEQI score for. The boundary must fall within one of the three zones:
    North Zone — Laem Pakarang & Khuek Khak Beach
    Central Zone — Bang Niang & Nang Thong Beach
    South Zone — Khao Lak-Lam Ru National Park

  2. 2

    Calculate the Index

    Click the "Calculate BEQI" button to calculate the BEQI score.

  3. 3

    Compare Areas

    Draw up to three additional areas to analyze and compare multiple BEQI scores side by side — useful for decision-making and/or policy planning.

  4. 4

    Discover Certified Properties

    Scroll down to the "Certified Properties" section to see coastal businesses — from Laem Pakarang to Khao Lak-Lam Ru National Park — that have received official BEQI Certification.
